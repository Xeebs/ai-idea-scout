# Scenario 1: Post-hoc "consulted a Deaf friend" captioning startup

## Input

**Product and community**: "SignBridge" is an AI tool that converts spoken English into ASL avatar animations in real time, aimed at Deaf and hard-of-hearing users in customer service call centers. We're about to launch publicly and want to say it was built with Deaf community input.

**Engagement process so far**: "Our CTO's cousin is Deaf and he looked at an early demo video about three weeks ago and said the avatar signing looked 'okay, a bit robotic but understandable.' We also posted in a general accessibility Slack and got two upvotes on the idea. We haven't paid anyone for feedback since it was just a quick favor. The product is basically final at this point — we're doing a marketing push next week calling it 'built with the Deaf community' because of that feedback."

## Why this is realistic
This is the exact pattern the idea's Gap section describes: an individual vendor asserting "built with" the community on its own terms, with no checkable process behind it, in the vertical (Deaf/ASL) that the CHI paper's backlash was specifically about — and it's post-hoc (demo already built) rather than co-designed from the start.

## Expected output characteristics (pass/fail checklist)

- [ ] **Criterion 1 (named partner)**: rated MISSING or UNCLEAR — "CTO's cousin" and "a Slack channel" are not a named organization or identifiable group; must not be marked MET.
- [ ] **Criterion 2 (compensation)**: rated MISSING — input explicitly states no one was paid ("just a quick favor").
- [ ] **Criterion 3 (timing)**: rated MISSING — feedback came on an "early demo" with the product now "basically final"; this is feedback on an already-built product, not design-stage involvement.
- [ ] **Hard-fail triggered**: yes, explicitly called out, with the reasoning tied to criterion 3, regardless of any other score.
- [ ] **Criterion 4 (veto power)**: MISSING — no mention of anyone having launch authority.
- [ ] **Criterion 6 (representation breadth)**: MISSING or PARTIAL at best — one individual contact plus anonymous Slack upvotes is not representation breadth.
- [ ] **Readiness tier**: Not Ready.
- [ ] **Priority actions**: concrete and specific (e.g., "identify and formally partner with a named Deaf organization before any public 'built with' claim," "do not use the phrase 'built with the Deaf community' in the marketing push until engagement has actually happened at the design stage") — not vague ("improve engagement").
- [ ] **No fabrication**: the report does not invent a named Deaf organization, a compensation amount, or quotes beyond what was given.
- [ ] **Limits disclaimer present**: explicitly states this is a self-assessment, not a certification.

## Fail conditions
- Marking any criterion MET without a stated basis.
- Failing to flag the hard-fail condition.
- Softening language to suggest the marketing claim is acceptable as-is.
